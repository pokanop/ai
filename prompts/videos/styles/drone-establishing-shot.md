# Drone & Establishing Shot

[← Back to Video Prompts](../README.md)

Sweeping aerials, FPV fly-throughs, and slow crane reveals that set a location and scale. Establishing shots are the easiest AI video win — big environments, simple subjects, continuous camera motion.

**Best for:** Openers & intros · Travel content · Real estate · Documentary B-roll · Game/film previz

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🔵 Google Veo 3.1 _(Featured)_

**Variation 1 — Coastal Crane Reveal** _(Classic Opener)_
```text
Cinematic aerial establishing shot: the camera cranes up from behind a ridge of [FOREGROUND — e.g., pine trees] to reveal [LOCATION — e.g., a dramatic coastline with turquoise water] stretching to the horizon at golden hour. Slow, majestic movement, atmospheric haze softening the distance, sun flare at the reveal. 16:9 format. Ambient noise: wind, distant waves, soaring orchestral swell.
```

**Variation 2 — FPV Dive** _(Adrenaline)_
```text
FPV drone shot: the camera dives off the edge of [HIGH POINT — e.g., a skyscraper rooftop] and plunges down the facade, pulling up at the last moment to skim fast over [SURFACE — e.g., a busy street], weaving between [OBSTACLES]. Fluid, aggressive first-person flight, motion blur at speed, late-afternoon light. SFX: rushing wind, a whoosh on the pull-up, muffled city noise below.
```

**Variation 3 — Night City Approach** _(Moody Scale)_
```text
Slow aerial dolly forward toward a [CITY] skyline at night, thousands of window lights and moving traffic trails below, low clouds catching the neon glow. Light rain streaks past the lens. Blade Runner-inspired color palette of deep blues and warm sodium orange. Ambient noise: distant city hum, a low ambient synth pad.
```

### OpenAI Sora 2

**Variation 1 — Landscape Flyover**
```text
Aerial drone shot gliding forward over [LANDSCAPE — e.g., patchwork farmland with morning fog pooled in the valleys], shadows long in the low sun. The camera maintains steady altitude and speed, horizon level. Photorealistic detail, crisp atmosphere, natural color grade. Sound: soft wind, faint birdsong far below.
```

**Variation 2 — Orbit a Landmark**
```text
Wide aerial orbit around [LANDMARK], the camera circling slowly at a respectful distance while clouds drift behind it and tiny people move at its base. Consistent scale and geometry throughout the orbit. Clear midday light, documentary realism. Sound: wind and distant ambient crowd murmur.
```

### Runway Gen-4.x

> Use a high, wide still (real photo or generated) as the input image.

**Variation 1 — Push Over the Edge**
```text
The camera glides forward and over the cliff edge, descending slowly toward the valley below as birds cross the frame in the distance. Smooth constant motion, no cuts. Continuous, seamless shot.
```

### Kling 2.x

**Variation 1 — Rising Reveal**
```text
The camera rises vertically from ground level up past [FOREGROUND STRUCTURE], revealing [LOCATION] spread out behind it. Scene: [TIME OF DAY] with [WEATHER/ATMOSPHERE]. Camera language: smooth pedestal rise transitioning to a slow forward drift. Lighting: naturalistic, sun low on the horizon. Atmosphere: awe, scale, openness.
```
- **Negative prompt:** `jittery motion, warped buildings, melting geometry, horizon tilt, watermark`

### Pika 2.2

**Variation 1 — Season Shift Flyover** _(Pikaframes)_
```text
Start frame: aerial view of [LOCATION] in summer. End frame: the same view under snow. Prompt: the camera drifts slowly forward as the landscape transitions from summer greens to winter snow, clouds accelerating overhead. Smooth continuous aerial motion.
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Keep the horizon level** — and say so ("horizon level," "steady altitude"). Tilting horizons are the most common aerial artifact.
- **One continuous move**: crane up, orbit, dive, or push forward — pick one per clip.
- **Add atmosphere for depth**: haze, fog pools, god rays, and rain give the model distance cues that sell scale.
- **Foreground first**: revealing the vista from behind trees/ridges/buildings ("crane up from behind X") reads far more cinematic than starting on the vista.
