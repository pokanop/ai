# Social / UGC Clip

[← Back to Video Prompts](../README.md)

Authentic, handheld, selfie-style content that feels like a real person filmed it on a phone — imperfect framing, natural light, direct-to-camera energy. The goal is credibility, not polish: these prompts deliberately avoid cinematic language.

**Best for:** TikTok & Reels ads · Creator-style testimonials · Product reactions · Vlogs · Organic-feel brand content

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🔵 Google Veo 3.1 _(Featured)_

> Veo 3.1's native dialogue makes it the strongest platform for talking-head UGC — script the exact line in quotes.

**Variation 1 — Selfie Testimonial** _(Creator Ad)_
```text
Vertical 9:16 selfie video, handheld front-facing phone camera with slight natural shake. [SUBJECT — e.g., a woman in her 20s with curly hair] sits in a bright, slightly messy bedroom with daylight from a window, holding [PRODUCT] up to the camera. She speaks directly to camera with casual, excited energy: "Okay so I've been using this for a week and [BENEFIT] — I'm honestly shocked." Natural skin texture, no studio lighting, authentic phone-video look. Ambient noise: quiet room tone.
```

**Variation 2 — Unboxing Reaction** _(Product Seeding)_
```text
Vertical 9:16 handheld phone video. Close on a pair of hands opening a [PACKAGE] on a kitchen counter, tilting up to reveal [SUBJECT]'s genuinely surprised face. They say, "No way — look at this," and hold [PRODUCT] closer to the lens, slightly out of focus before the camera refocuses. Warm indoor lighting, casual realism. SFX: cardboard tearing, paper rustling, a short delighted laugh.
```

**Variation 3 — POV Walk-and-Talk** _(Vlog Energy)_
```text
Vertical 9:16 selfie video, arm extended, walking outdoors down [LOCATION — e.g., a city sidewalk]. [SUBJECT] talks to camera between glances at the street: "So here's the thing about [TOPIC]…" Natural sunlight with moving shadows, background pedestrians softly blurred, real handheld bounce in the frame. Ambient noise: street sounds, wind on the mic.
```

### OpenAI Sora 2

**Variation 1 — Get-Ready-With-Me Beat**
```text
Vertical smartphone video, mirror POV: [SUBJECT] gets ready in a bathroom mirror, applying [PRODUCT] while talking casually to their reflection and the camera: "[SHORT CASUAL LINE]". Slightly overexposed window light, authentic phone-camera color, small focus hunts. Sound: room echo on the voice, tap water briefly running.
```

**Variation 2 — Street Interview**
```text
Vertical handheld video on a busy sidewalk: an interviewer's hand holds a microphone toward [SUBJECT], who laughs and answers, "[ANSWER LINE]". Passersby glance at the camera. Overcast daylight, realistic crowd noise, authentic man-on-the-street energy.
```

### Runway Gen-4.x

**Variation 1 — Animate a Creator Still**
```text
Handheld selfie-video motion: the subject talks animatedly to the camera, gesturing with one hand, natural head movement and blinking. The camera drifts and shakes slightly like an arm-held phone. Background pedestrians move past. Authentic smartphone video look.
```

### Kling 2.x

**Variation 1 — Casual Product Demo**
```text
[SUBJECT] holds [PRODUCT] toward the camera, rotating it and pointing at a feature while nodding and smiling. Scene: a bright everyday kitchen, morning window light. Camera language: handheld phone framing at arm's length, subtle shake. Lighting: natural daylight, no studio polish. Atmosphere: casual, friendly, authentic.
```
- **Negative prompt:** `studio lighting, cinematic grade, tripod-smooth motion, warped hands, distorted face`

### Pika 2.2

**Variation 1 — Reaction Insert** _(Pikadditions-friendly)_
```text
Vertical 9:16 handheld clip: [SUBJECT] does a double-take at [OBJECT] on the table, leans in close, eyes widening, then grins at the camera. Natural window light, phone-video texture, slight motion blur on the lean-in.
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Imperfection is the style**: Ask for "slight natural shake," "small focus hunts," "no studio lighting" — polish kills the UGC read.
- **Script the hook**: On audio platforms (Veo, Sora), the first spoken line IS the hook. Write it in quotes, keep it under ~15 words for a 8s clip.
- **9:16 always**: Shoot vertical for TikTok/Reels/Shorts; specify it explicitly.
- **Cast the creator**: Describe age range, style, and energy of the on-camera subject — "a guy in his 30s in a hoodie, deadpan delivery" is a casting note.
- **Disclose AI use**: Platforms increasingly require labeling synthetic people in ads — keep it in mind for paid placements.
