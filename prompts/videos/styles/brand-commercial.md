# Brand Commercial

[← Back to Video Prompts](../README.md)

Polished 15–30 second spot energy compressed into single clips: lifestyle moments, brand-world montage beats, logo-adjacent moods. Commercials live and die on grade, casting, and pacing — prompt all three explicitly.

**Best for:** Brand spots · Pitch decks & mood films · Pre-roll ads · Agency previz · Landing page heroes

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🔵 Google Veo 3.1 _(Featured)_

**Variation 1 — Lifestyle Moment** _(Warm & Human)_
```text
Commercial lifestyle shot: [PERSON — e.g., a woman in her 30s in a linen shirt] laughs while [ACTIVITY — e.g., pouring coffee for friends] in a sun-drenched [SETTING — e.g., Scandinavian kitchen], slow dolly-in as morning light blooms through sheer curtains. Warm editorial grade, soft skin tones, gentle lens flare. Ambient noise: warm room tone, soft laughter, an acoustic guitar bed.
```

**Variation 2 — Athletic Energy** _(Sports Brand)_
```text
High-energy commercial shot: [ATHLETE] explodes off the line in [SETTING — e.g., an empty stadium tunnel at dawn], the camera tracking low and fast alongside, breath fogging, fabric of their [PRODUCT/APPAREL] rippling in crisp detail. Desaturated grade with one bold accent color, dramatic rim light. SFX: sharp breathing, footfalls with heavy sub-bass hits, a rising riser.
```

**Variation 3 — Tabletop Food** _(Appetite Appeal)_
```text
Macro tabletop commercial shot: [FOOD/DRINK — e.g., honey drizzling over golden pancakes] in extreme slow motion, steam curling, glossy highlights rolling across the surface as the camera arcs slowly. Rich warm grade, dark moody background, single hard key light. SFX: intimate drizzle and sizzle foley, no music.
```

### OpenAI Sora 2

**Variation 1 — Multi-Shot Brand Montage**
```text
Three-shot commercial montage for a [BRAND CATEGORY — e.g., outdoor apparel brand]. Shot 1: sunrise wide of a hiker on a ridgeline. Shot 2: close-up of hands cinching a pack strap, fabric texture crisp. Shot 3: slow-motion stride through frame with alpine backdrop, jacket in [BRAND COLOR]. Consistent cool-warm grade across shots, aspirational tone. Sound: wind, fabric, one swelling cinematic chord.
```

**Variation 2 — Problem/Solution Beat**
```text
Commercial vignette: [PERSON] struggles with [RELATABLE PROBLEM — e.g., a tangle of chargers] with mild comic frustration, then a match cut to the same scene transformed — clean, calm, organized around [PRODUCT PLACEHOLDER]. Bright, airy grade, playful timing. Sound: cluttered foley resolving into a single satisfying click and a light musical button.
```

### Runway Gen-4.x

> Shoot or generate a product/lifestyle still on-brand, then add motion.

**Variation 1 — Living Key Visual**
```text
Cinematic slow push-in as subtle motion brings the scene alive: hair and fabric moving in a light breeze, background elements drifting softly, light shifting warmer. Premium commercial polish, no camera shake. Continuous, seamless shot.
```

### Kling 2.x

**Variation 1 — Fragrance-Ad Mood**
```text
A [MODEL] in [WARDROBE] turns slowly through a beam of light in a dark space, silk fabric floating around them in slow motion, gold particles drifting. Scene: minimalist black studio. Camera language: slow 180-degree orbit. Lighting: single dramatic shaft with deep shadow. Atmosphere: luxurious, dreamlike, editorial.
```
- **Negative prompt:** `distorted face, extra fingers, cheap lighting, jitter, watermark`

### Pika 2.2

**Variation 1 — Brand Color Takeover** _(Pikaframes)_
```text
Start frame: a muted grey [SCENE — e.g., city street]. End frame: the same scene washed in [BRAND COLOR] accents — umbrellas, signage, wardrobe. Prompt: the brand color blooms across the scene element by element in a smooth wave, camera holding steady. Playful, saturated grade.
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Grade = brand**: name the color language ("warm editorial," "desaturated with one accent color") — it's the fastest way to feel on-brand.
- **Use product placeholders**: AI-rendered logos and labels are unreliable; frame around a `[PRODUCT]` stand-in and comp the real pack shot in post.
- **One emotion per clip**: joy, grit, calm, appetite. Montage multiple clips for the full spot arc.
- **Foley sells premium**: intimate SFX (drizzle, fabric, a click) reads more expensive than wall-to-wall music.
