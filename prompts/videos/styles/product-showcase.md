# Product Showcase

[← Back to Video Prompts](../README.md)

Studio-quality product reveals with orbit shots, macro detail passes, and clean controlled lighting. These prompts reproduce the language of high-end commercial tabletop photography — seamless backgrounds, specular highlights, and hero moments.

**Best for:** E-commerce videos · Launch teasers · App store previews · Kickstarter pages · Social ads

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🔵 Google Veo 3.1 _(Featured)_

**Variation 1 — 360 Orbit Hero Shot** _(Launch Teaser)_
```text
Studio product video: a slow, smooth 360-degree orbit shot around [PRODUCT] resting on a [SURFACE MATERIAL] pedestal against a seamless [COLOR] background. Soft diffused key light with a crisp rim light tracing the product's edges, subtle specular highlights rolling across its surface as the camera circles. Shallow depth of field, macro-level sharpness on the product. Premium, minimal aesthetic. SFX: a soft airy whoosh building to a clean resolve tone.
```

**Variation 2 — Macro Detail Pass** _(Craftsmanship Story)_
```text
Extreme macro tracking shot gliding across the surface of [PRODUCT], revealing [MATERIAL DETAIL — e.g., brushed aluminum grain, stitched leather, condensation droplets]. Dramatic side lighting rakes across the texture. The camera drifts slowly, focus locked, background falling to black. Luxurious, tactile mood. Ambient noise: subtle, deep ambient hum with delicate high-frequency shimmer.
```

**Variation 3 — Splash / Dynamic Reveal** _(High Energy)_
```text
High-speed slow-motion shot: [PRODUCT] drops into frame and lands on a reflective black surface as a burst of [ELEMENT — e.g., water splash, powder, citrus slices] erupts around it, droplets frozen mid-air. Hard backlight makes the burst glow, product remains tack sharp at center. 16:9 format. SFX: a deep impact thud with sparkling particle sounds.
```

### OpenAI Sora 2

**Variation 1 — Lifestyle Context Shot**
```text
A sunlit kitchen counter in the morning; a hand reaches in and picks up [PRODUCT], turning it slightly so the label catches the window light. The camera slowly dollies in as steam or ambient dust drifts through the light. Photorealistic, shallow depth of field, warm natural grade. Sound: quiet morning ambience, a soft clink as the product is set down.
```

**Variation 2 — Exploded Feature Tour**
```text
Studio shot on a seamless white cyclorama: [PRODUCT] floats at center frame and gently separates into its component parts, each part rotating slowly in place, then reassembles seamlessly. Clean soft lighting, subtle reflections on the floor. Precise, engineered motion. Sound: minimal electronic ticks synchronized with each part separating.
```

### Runway Gen-4.x

> Upload a clean product still — the prompt animates it.

**Variation 1 — Orbit from Still**
```text
The camera slowly orbits the product counterclockwise as soft specular highlights roll across its surface. The background gradient shifts subtly. Locked focus on the product, smooth constant speed. Continuous, seamless shot.
```

**Variation 2 — Light Sweep**
```text
Locked camera. A soft beam of light sweeps across the product from left to right, revealing its contours, while faint particles drift in the background. The product remains perfectly still and sharp.
```

### Kling 2.x

**Variation 1 — Pedestal Reveal**
```text
[PRODUCT] rises slowly from below frame on a rotating pedestal, stopping at center. Scene: minimalist studio with a seamless [COLOR] backdrop and soft floor reflection. Camera language: locked shot with a slight slow zoom-in. Lighting: soft key with edge rim light. Atmosphere: premium, clean, modern.
```

### Pika 2.2

**Variation 1 — Before/After Transformation** _(Pikaframes)_
```text

```

## 💡 Tips

- **Protect the label**: Logos and text warp easily. Keep the product still or slow-moving, and use image-to-video (Runway/Kling/Pika) or Veo's reference images to lock its appearance.
- **Reflections sell**: Ask for "soft floor reflection" or "specular highlights rolling across the surface" — motion in the highlights makes products feel physical.
- **One hero move**: An orbit OR a macro pass OR a splash. Combining them in one 5–8s clip muddies the shot.
- **Match ratios to placement**: 9:16 for Stories/TikTok ads, 1:1 for feeds, 16:9 for landing pages.
- **Pairs well with:** [Brand Commercial](brand-commercial.md), [Timelapse & Hyperlapse](timelapse-hyperlapse.md)
