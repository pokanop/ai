# Timelapse & Hyperlapse

[← Back to Video Prompts](../README.md)

Compressed time: cities pulsing at night, clouds streaming past monuments, construction rising, seasons turning. Timelapse prompts trade subject motion for environmental motion — the world moves, the camera holds (timelapse) or glides (hyperlapse).

**Best for:** Intros & transitions · Travel content · Corporate sizzle reels · Music video B-roll · Ambient screens

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🔵 Google Veo 3.1 _(Featured)_

**Variation 1 — City Rush Hour** _(Classic)_
```text
Timelapse from a high rooftop overlooking [CITY INTERSECTION]: traffic streams as continuous light trails, pedestrians blur into flowing rivers of motion, clouds race across the sky as day compresses into dusk and windows flicker on across the skyline. Locked camera, sharp architecture, saturated dusk gradient. Ambient noise: accelerated city hum with a rhythmic pulse.
```

**Variation 2 — Storm Cell Build** _(Weather)_
```text
Timelapse of a supercell thunderstorm building over [LANDSCAPE — e.g., open prairie]: towering cumulus boiling upward in accelerated motion, the anvil spreading, lightning flickering inside the cloud mass as the base darkens. Locked wide shot, dramatic scale contrast with tiny [FOREGROUND ELEMENT]. Ambient noise: deep rumbling wind, distant thunder.
```

**Variation 3 — Urban Hyperlapse** _(Moving Camera)_
```text
Hyperlapse gliding forward down [STREET/LANDMARK APPROACH — e.g., a neon-lit shopping street], the camera advancing smoothly while pedestrians and traffic streak past in accelerated motion, signage flickering, sky shifting from blue hour to full night. Stabilized forward motion, perspective lines converging ahead. Ambient noise: whooshing accelerated crowd, rhythmic electronic pulse.
```

### OpenAI Sora 2

**Variation 1 — Nature's Clock**
```text
Timelapse: shadows sweep across [LANDSCAPE — e.g., a canyon] as the sun arcs overhead, light changing from cold dawn blue through hard midday to amber sunset, stars finally wheeling into view above the silhouetted rim. Locked ultra-wide shot, seamless light transitions, photorealistic. Sound: slowly evolving ambient drone.
```

**Variation 2 — Construction Rise**
```text
Timelapse of [STRUCTURE — e.g., a timber-frame house] being built: foundation, framing, roof, and finish appearing in accelerated stages, workers as fast-moving blurs, materials arriving and vanishing, clouds streaming overhead through changing weather. Locked wide shot from a fixed corner angle. Sound: accelerated construction rhythm — hammering, saws, engines.
```

### Runway Gen-4.x

```text
The clouds race across the sky in accelerated timelapse motion while the foreground remains sharp and static, light and shadows sweeping across the scene as the sun moves. Locked camera. Continuous, seamless shot.
```

### Kling 2.x

**Variation 1 — Seasons Turn**
```text
A single [TREE/LANDMARK] as the seasons cycle in timelapse: green summer leaves flare to autumn orange, fall away, snow blankets the branches, then buds and blossoms return. Scene: [SETTING], locked framing throughout. Camera language: static wide shot. Lighting: shifting naturally with each season. Atmosphere: the quiet passage of time.

```

## 💡 Tips

- **Lock the camera** (or name one smooth hyperlapse path) — accidental drift ruins the timelapse illusion.
- **Anchor with something static**: a monument, tree, or skyline holds the frame together while everything else streams.
- **Describe motion as trails and streams**: "light trails," "flowing rivers of pedestrians," "clouds racing" cue the compressed-time look.
- **Light transitions are the money shot**: day-to-dusk and blue-hour-to-night grade shifts read as premium footage.
