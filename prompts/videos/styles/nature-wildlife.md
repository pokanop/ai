# Nature & Wildlife

[← Back to Video Prompts](../README.md)

Documentary-grade animal behavior, macro nature detail, and dramatic weather. Nature is a model strength — organic motion hides small artifacts — but animal anatomy and gait still reward careful, specific prompting.

**Best for:** Documentary B-roll · Screensavers & ambient loops · Educational content · Stock footage · Meditation apps

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🔵 Google Veo 3.1 _(Featured)_

**Variation 1 — Apex Predator Portrait** _(BBC Style)_
```text
Wildlife documentary telephoto shot: a [ANIMAL — e.g., snow leopard] moves slowly along a [TERRAIN — e.g., rocky Himalayan ridge], muscles shifting under its coat, breath visible in the cold air. It pauses and looks directly toward the lens. Compressed telephoto perspective, creamy background blur, natural overcast light. Ambient noise: wind over rock, faint crunch of paws on gravel, no music.
```

**Variation 2 — Macro Behavior** _(Intimate)_
```text
Extreme macro shot: a [SMALL SUBJECT — e.g., honeybee] lands on a [FLOWER], pollen grains clinging to its legs as it works, antennae twitching. Dew droplets on the petals refract the morning sun. Razor-thin focus plane, gentle rack focus following the movement. SFX: soft wing buzz, faint birdsong in the distance.
```

**Variation 3 — Storm Landscape** _(Drama)_
```text
Wide locked-off shot of [LANDSCAPE — e.g., a lone oak on a grassy hill] as a storm front rolls in: clouds churning, grass whipping in gusts, a distant lightning strike illuminating the frame for a beat. Color shifts from warm to cold steel-grey as the front passes. Ambient noise: rising wind, rolling thunder, first heavy raindrops.
```

### OpenAI Sora 2

**Variation 1 — Herd Migration Aerial**
```text
Aerial tracking shot following a herd of [ANIMALS — e.g., wildebeest] streaming across [TERRAIN — e.g., a dusty river delta], dust rising in their wake, the herd flowing around obstacles like a current. Golden hour light, long shadows, documentary realism with accurate herd dynamics. Sound: rumbling hooves, distant lowing, wind.
```

**Variation 2 — Underwater Encounter**
```text
Underwater shot: a [SEA CREATURE — e.g., manta ray] glides directly over the camera in clear blue water, sunbeams shafting down from the surface, a school of small fish parting around it. Slow, weightless motion, particles drifting in the light. Sound: muffled underwater ambience, soft bubble trails.
```

### Runway Gen-4.x

> Start from a strong wildlife still — telephoto photos animate especially well.

**Variation 1 — Bring a Photo to Life**
```text
The animal turns its head slowly toward the camera, ears twitching, wind moving through its fur, background grass swaying gently. Subtle, naturalistic motion only. Continuous, seamless shot.
```

### Kling 2.x

**Variation 1 — Forest Reveal**
```text
A [ANIMAL — e.g., red fox] steps out of morning mist between ferns, pauses alert, then trots across the frame. Scene: old-growth forest at dawn, god rays through the canopy. Camera language: static wide shot, letting the animal cross frame. Lighting: soft volumetric dawn light. Atmosphere: quiet, primeval stillness.
```
- **Negative prompt:** `extra legs, deformed anatomy, unnatural gait, morphing fur, watermark`

## 💡 Tips

- **Name the behavior, not just the animal**: "pauses alert, ears rotating" reads truer than "a fox in a forest."
- **Telephoto compression + shallow focus** is the universal documentary look — say both.
- **Gait is the tell**: for walking/running animals, slow movement and pauses hide gait artifacts; full-speed gallops are risky.
- **Weather is free production value**: mist, dust, rain, and wind add motion and mood everywhere in frame.
- **Skip music for realism**: natural ambience (wind, hooves, birdsong) sells the documentary feel better than a score.
