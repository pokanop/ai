# Podcast & Jingle

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

Intros, outros, stingers, and branded audio idents — short-form music with a job to do, plus the voice reads that go over them. Combines Suno/Udio for the music bed and ElevenLabs v3 for the host read.

**Best for:** Podcast intros/outros · YouTube channel idents · Radio imaging · Brand sonic logos · Segment stingers

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🟠 Suno v5 _(Featured — Music Beds)_

Style of Music:
```text
upbeat podcast intro theme, 110 BPM, punchy drums, funky bass, bright electric piano stabs, hand claps, energetic and friendly, clean modern production, instrumental, 30 second theme
```

Lyrics (structure only):
```text
[Intro]
[Instrumental]
[Outro]
[End]
```

```text
modern tech podcast theme, 100 BPM, minimal electronic pulse, clean synth plucks, subtle glitch percussion, confident and forward-looking, sleek corporate energy, instrumental
```

Style of Music:
```text
catchy advertising jingle, 120 BPM, bright acoustic guitar, hand claps, cheerful group vocals, singalong melody, retro commercial charm
```

Lyrics:
```text
[Chorus]
[TAGLINE LINE ONE — e.g., "When you need it fast and right"]
[BRAND HOOK — e.g., "[BRAND NAME] makes it easy!"]
[End]
```

### 🟣 ElevenLabs v3 _(Host Read Over the Bed)_

**Variation 1 — Podcast Cold Open**
```text
[excited] Welcome back to [SHOW NAME] — the show where [ONE-LINE PREMISE]. I'm [HOST NAME].

[curious] Today: [EPISODE HOOK — one intriguing sentence]. [pause] Trust me — you'll want to hear how this ends.

[cheerfully] Let's get into it.
```

**Variation 2 — Outro & Call to Action**
```text
[warmly] And that's it for today's episode. If you enjoyed it, [pause] you know the drill — follow the show, leave a review, and share it with someone who'd love it.

[cheerfully] Next week: [TEASER]. [pause] See you then.
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Ask for a length**: "30 second theme" nudges the arrangement toward a tight ident instead of a full song.
- **Keep jingles to one hook**: a single sung tagline repeated beats a full verse/chorus for brand recall.
- **Duck the bed**: generate music and voice separately, then mix with the bed lowered under the read.
- **Stingers are 3–7 seconds**: ask for "short ident" and a "hit at the end" for clean button endings.
