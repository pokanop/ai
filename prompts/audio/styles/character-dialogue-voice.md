# Character & Dialogue Voice

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

Expressive character performances with ElevenLabs v3 — emotional range, non-verbal sounds, and multi-character scenes. Push the audio tags harder here than in narration: characters can laugh, whisper, shout, and break.

**Best for:** Game characters · Animation dubbing · Audio dramas · Character-driven ads · Interactive agents

---

## Prompt Variations

### 🟣 ElevenLabs v3 _(Featured)_

**Variation 1 — Emotional Range Scene** _(One Character, Full Arc)_
```text
[calm] So… you came back. [pause] I didn't think you would.

[curious] Wait. What's that in your hand? [gasps] Is that — [excited] you actually found it? You FOUND it?

[laughs] I can't believe this. After all these years! [warmly] Come here. Come here — let me look at you.
```

**Variation 2 — Villain Monologue** _(Menace & Control)_
```text
[calm] Did you really think it would be that easy? [pause]

[sarcastic] "Break into the vault," they said. "He'll never notice," they said. [laughs]

[whispers] I noticed the moment you touched the door. [pause] [cold] And now… we're going to have a little conversation about what you owe me.
```

**Variation 3 — Comedic Panic** _(High Energy)_
```text
[nervously] Okay. Okay okay okay. This is fine. Everything is FINE. [rushed] The [OBJECT] is missing, the [EVENT] starts in ten minutes, and I may have — [clears throat] — slightly set the kitchen on fire.

[gasps] Wait. WAIT. [excited] I have an idea. [pause] [sheepishly] You're not going to like it.
```

**Variation 4 — Two-Character Exchange** _(Generate per voice)_

> Generate each character with its own voice, then edit together — or use ElevenLabs' dialogue mode where available.

Character A:
```text
[weary] We've been walking for hours. Admit it — we're lost.
```
Character B:
```text
[confident] We are not lost. [pause] [quietly] We are… temporarily unlocated.
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Escalate through tags**: `[calm] → [curious] → [excited]` in one passage creates a believable emotional arc.
- **Non-verbals sell character**: `[laughs]`, `[gasps]`, `[sighs]`, `[clears throat]` make performances feel acted, not read.
- **Match tag to voice temperament**: a soft-spoken voice does `[whispers]` beautifully and fights `[shouting]` — cast accordingly.
- **One voice per character**: generate separately per character for scenes; keep each character's lines in their own request.
