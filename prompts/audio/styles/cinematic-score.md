# Cinematic Score

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

Orchestral and hybrid trailer music, emotional themes, and underscore beds for film, trailers, and games. Prompt like a music supervisor: name the emotion, the instrumentation, and the arc.

**Best for:** Trailers & teasers · Film/game underscore · Brand films · Podcast beds · Presentation openers

## 💡 Tips

- **Name the emotional arc**: "builds from quiet tension to overwhelming climax" shapes the whole arrangement.
- **Reference orchestration, not songs**: "staccato string ostinato", "soaring brass theme", "felt piano" beat vague words like "epic music".
- **Keep it instrumental**: add "instrumental" explicitly or you may get unwanted vocals; use "choir swells" for voices as texture.
- **Underscore ≠ theme**: for background beds ask for "sparse", "slow evolving", "no strong melody" so it sits under dialogue.
