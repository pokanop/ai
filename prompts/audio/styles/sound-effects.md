# Sound Effects

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

Foley, ambience, impacts, and UI sounds. SFX prompts are short and physical: describe the source, the action, the space it happens in, and the character of the sound. Prompts below target ElevenLabs Sound Effects; the same descriptions work as inline `SFX:` cues in Veo 3.1 and Sora 2 video prompts.

**Best for:** Video sound design · Game audio · App/UI sounds · Podcast production · Ambience beds

---

## 💡 Tips

- **One event per prompt**: "door creak" and "footsteps" as separate generations mix better than one crowded prompt.
- **Name the space**: "in a stone cathedral", "cozy interior perspective" — acoustic space controls reverb and distance.
- **Describe character, not decibels**: "sharp", "warm", "tactile", "airy" steer the timbre effectively.
- **Say "seamless loop"** for ambience beds you intend to repeat.
- **Keep UI sounds short**: add "short" or "under one second" or you'll get lingering tails.
- **Pairs well with:** [Cinematic Score](cinematic-score.md), [Lo-fi & Ambient](lofi-ambient.md)
