# Electronic & Dance

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

House, techno, synthwave, and EDM — genres where BPM, drum character, and synth palette do most of the work. These prompts lean on precise tempo and instrumentation tags; most work as instrumentals.

**Best for:** DJ sets & mixes · Game and stream soundtracks · Workout playlists · Product launch videos · Fashion content

## 💡 Tips

- **Always state BPM**: it's the single strongest control for electronic genres — 122–126 house/techno, ~100 synthwave, 128 big room, 170+ DnB.
- **Name the drum character**: "four-on-the-floor kick", "shuffled hi-hats", "rolling breakbeats" define the genre as much as the label does.
- **Use `[Build]` and `[Drop]` tags**: they map directly onto dance arrangement and reliably shape the energy curve.
- **Instrumental by default**: add "instrumental" to the style unless you specifically want vocals.
- **Pairs well with:** [Pop Anthem](pop-anthem.md), [Lo-fi & Ambient](lofi-ambient.md)
