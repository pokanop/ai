# Pop Anthem

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

Radio-ready pop songs with big hooks, clean modern production, and full verse/chorus structure. The featured Suno prompts pair a dense style description with structured custom lyrics.

**Best for:** Original songs · Brand anthems · Event themes · Social soundtracks · Demos for writers

## 💡 Tips

- **Hook first**: write the chorus hook line before anything else — everything serves it.
- **Keep lines singable**: 6–10 syllables per line lands most naturally; long prose lines get rushed.
- **Contrast sections**: intimate verses ("minimal", "stripped") make choruses ("huge", "wide", "layered") hit harder.
- **One `[Bridge]`, late**: a stripped-back bridge before the final chorus is the classic pop lift.
