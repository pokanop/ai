# Lo-fi & Ambient

[← Back to Audio Prompts](../README.md)

Chill beats, ambient textures, and long-form soundscapes for focus, sleep, and background listening. These genres reward texture words — tape hiss, vinyl crackle, room tone — over melody direction.

**Best for:** Focus/study playlists · Sleep & meditation apps · Stream backgrounds · Café/retail ambience · App soundscapes

## 💡 Tips

- **Texture is the genre**: vinyl crackle, tape hiss, rain, room tone — name at least one texture layer.
- **Say "beatless"** for pure ambient; otherwise a beat usually appears.
- **Slow BPM anchors the mood**: 70–90 BPM for lo-fi; omit BPM entirely for free-floating ambient.
- **Loop-friendly**: ask for "slowly evolving" and avoid big builds if you need seamless background loops.
- **Pairs well with:** [Cinematic Score](cinematic-score.md), [Sound Effects](sound-effects.md)
